BEEB-LLM

A transformer language model running on a 1981 BBC Micro. Character-level transformers from 10K to 50K parameters (int8 weights, a 32-character sliding context) trained on a public-domain stand-in (Jerome K. Jerome's Three Men in a Boat and Three Men on the Bummel) type out new text, in pure 6502 machine code, in jsbeeb. At boot it looks for a 6502 Second Processor, shows a menu of the models that fit, and starts the best one after five seconds: the 17K "Small" model on a plain Model B (about 1.6 characters a second), the 35K "Medium" one on a second processor. Click the emulator to give it the keyboard.

The status line shows the temperature, top-k and the measured speed in characters (tokens) per second. The model only knows lower case; the Beeb capitalises sentences and a lone "i" as it types.
